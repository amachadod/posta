## Needs you
- **Client requests**: a change, an extra deliverable, a question about results. Priority high if the client sounds unhappy.
- **Deadlines the client has heard**: launch dates, report dates, approvals they're waiting on from you.
- **Approvals from clients**: creative or budget approvals arriving or overdue. Create a task to act on the approval.
- **Suppliers and freelancers** delivering work that you need to review before it goes to the client.
- **Risk signals**: complaints, a new decision maker, budget cuts, "we're reviewing our agencies". Priority high, task to plan a response.

## Tasks
Title format: verb + what + client. Example: "Send revised media plan to Casa Verde Hotels".
Put what the client asked for, in their words, in the evidence.

## Draft replies
Clients get a reply the same day, even if it's only "got it, I'll come back by Thursday". Acknowledge, say what happens next and when.
