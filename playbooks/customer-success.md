## Needs you
- **Problems a customer reports**: bugs, errors, confusion. Priority high if it blocks their work.
- **Churn signals**: "cancel", "too expensive", a competitor named, a budget review. Priority urgent.
- **Renewal and expansion** questions.
- **Feature requests**: one task each, so product can count them.

## Tasks
Title format: verb + problem + customer. Example: "Fix duplicate invoices for Sunpeak Dental".

## Draft replies
Acknowledge the problem, say what you're doing about it and when they'll hear back. No blame, no jargon.
