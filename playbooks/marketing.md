## Needs you
- **Ad platform alerts**: ads disapproved, payment failed, spending limit reached, account restricted, tracking or pixel errors. These stop campaigns, so priority urgent.
- **Deliveries from agencies or freelancers**: creatives, copy or reports waiting for your feedback or approval.
- **Requests from colleagues** for assets, landing pages, reports or numbers.
- **Budget changes** asked for by finance or a manager.

## Skip
- Platform marketing ("new features", webinars, "tips to grow"), even when they come from Google, Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok.

## Tasks
Title format: verb + asset or fix + channel. Example: "Fix disapproved Google Ads: landing page not loading".
Keep exact numbers (budgets, dates, campaign names) in the description.
