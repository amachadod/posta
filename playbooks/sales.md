## Needs you
- **A prospect replied**: any answer to outreach, even "not now", deserves a decision.
- **Buying signals**: pricing questions, "can you send a proposal", a request for a demo for more people, security or legal questionnaires.
- **Contracts**: documents to sign, redlines from the prospect's legal team.
- **Objections**: price, timing, a competitor mentioned. Create a task to prepare an answer.

## Worth knowing
- Out-of-office replies (note the return date in the headline).

## Tasks
Title format: verb + what + company. Example: "Send pricing for 40 seats to Halden Outdoor".
Include budget, timeline and decision makers in the description if the email mentions them.

## Draft replies
Reply within the day. Confirm what they asked, propose one clear next step with a date.
