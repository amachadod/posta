How I write emails (edit this so drafts sound like you):

- Short and friendly. Two to five sentences is usually enough.
- Start with the person's first name ("Hi Sofía,"). No "I hope this email finds you well".
- Answer the question first, then the next step and when it will happen.
- Plain words, no corporate phrases like "circle back" or "per my last email".
- Reply in the language the email was written in.
- Sign off with "Thanks," or "Best," and my first name.
